怎么切换语言？

首页最下方有一个语言下拉，默认跟随系统语言。阅读过程中不切换。

怎么往下读？

轻触屏幕任意位置继续。文字还在逐字出现时轻触一下，会直接显示整段。

「继续阅读」和「重新开始」有什么区别？

「继续阅读」从你上次读到的那封信的开头接着读。

「重新开始」从本轮的第一封信重新读起。本轮的选择会重置，但已经发现的结局和阅读进度都会保留。

中途退出会丢进度吗？

不会。下次会从你当时那封信的开头重新读起。

一共有几个结局？

三个。第一次读完只能到达其中一个，其余的要在后续的阅读里才有机会看到。已经到达的结局收在首页最下方的「结局图鉴」里。读完一个结局后，首页会开放下一轮阅读。

我读了两遍，还是同一个结局

第二次读的时候，请读得慢一点。如果某个字看起来不太对，相信那种感觉——你可以轻触它。你怎么回信、怎么处置寄来的东西，也都算数。

需要联网吗？

不需要。这个游戏完全离线运行，在飞行模式下也能玩。

换了手机，进度还在吗？

进度只保存在当前设备上，不会在设备之间同步。用同一个 Apple 账户在新手机上重新下载本 App 不需要再付费，但需要从头开始读。

删除数据

所有数据都保存在你的设备上。删除这个 App 会删除它的全部本地数据；你的系统备份可能保留较早的副本。

How do I change the language?

There is a language selector at the bottom of the home screen. It follows your system language by default. The language does not change mid-reading.

How do I keep reading?

Tap anywhere on the screen to continue. Tapping while the text is still appearing shows the whole paragraph at once.

What is the difference between Continue Reading and Restart?

Continue Reading returns to the start of the letter you had reached.

Restart begins again from the first letter of this reading. The choices you made in this reading are cleared, but the endings you have found and your progress through the readings are kept.

Will I lose my place if I close the app?

No. You will return to the beginning of the letter you were reading.

How many endings are there?

Three. Your first reading can reach only one of them; the others become possible in later readings. Endings you have reached are kept in the Ending Gallery, at the bottom of the home screen. Once you finish an ending, the home screen opens the next reading.

I have read it twice and reached the same ending

On your second reading, go slowly. If a word looks wrong, trust that feeling — you can tap it. How you reply, and what you do with the things he sends you, matter too.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game runs entirely offline, including in Airplane Mode.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Progress is stored on the current device only and does not sync. You can download the app again on a new iPhone with the same Apple Account at no charge, but you will begin reading from the start.

Deleting your data

Everything is stored on your device. Removing the app removes all of its local data; operating-system backups may retain earlier copies.
