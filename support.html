怎么切换语言？

在标题页左下角，点「中文」或「EN」。

怎么往下读？

轻触屏幕任意位置继续。文字还在逐字出现时轻触一下，会直接显示整段。

「继续阅读」和「拆开新信」有什么区别？

「继续阅读」从你上次停下的地方接着读。

「拆开新信」会从头开始，并清空全部进度——包括周目数和已经解锁的结局。如果你只是想接着读，请点「继续阅读」。

一共有几个结局？

三个。第一次读完只能到达其中一个，其余的要在第二次阅读时才有机会看到。已经到达的结局收在标题页右下角的「结局图鉴」里。

我读了两遍，还是同一个结局

第二次读的时候，请读得慢一点。如果某个字看起来不太对，相信那种感觉——你可以轻触它。你怎么回信、怎么处置寄来的东西，也都算数。

需要联网吗？

不需要。这个游戏完全离线运行，在飞行模式下也能玩。

换了手机，进度还在吗？

进度只保存在当前设备上，不会在设备之间同步。用同一个 Apple 账户在新手机上重新下载本 App 不需要再付费，但需要从头开始读。

删除数据

所有数据都保存在你的设备上。删除这个 App 会删除它的全部本地数据；你的系统备份可能保留较早的副本。

How do I change the language?

On the title screen, tap “中文” or “EN” in the bottom-left corner.

How do I keep reading?

Tap anywhere on the screen to continue. Tapping while the text is still appearing shows the whole paragraph at once.

What is the difference between Continue Reading and Open New Letter?

Continue Reading picks up where you left off.

Open New Letter starts again from the beginning and clears all progress — including which reading you are on and the endings you have unlocked. If you only want to carry on, choose Continue Reading.

How many endings are there?

Three. Your first reading can reach only one of them; the others become possible on a second reading. Endings you have reached are kept in the Ending Gallery, at the bottom right of the title screen.

I have read it twice and reached the same ending

On your second reading, go slowly. If a word looks wrong, trust that feeling — you can tap it. How you reply, and what you do with the things he sends you, matter too.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game runs entirely offline, including in Airplane Mode.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Progress is stored on the current device only and does not sync. You can download the app again on a new iPhone with the same Apple Account at no charge, but you will begin reading from the start.

Deleting your data

Everything is stored on your device. Removing the app removes all of its local data; operating-system backups may retain earlier copies.
