中文

《不可回信》不会把任何信息发送给我们，也不会发送给任何第三方。这个 App 完全离线运行，不建立任何网络连接，没有账号，没有广告，没有统计分析，也不做任何形式的追踪。

只存在你手机上的东西

游戏需要记住的内容，全部只保存在你的设备上：

  • 你为收信人填写的名字
  • 你读到了第几封信，以及你在信中做出的选择
  • 当前是第几周目、已经解锁了哪些结局
  • 你选择的语言

你填写的名字只用来在故事里称呼你，可以是任何名字，不必是真名。以上内容都不会离开你的设备，我们也无从读取。

删除这个 App 会同时删除这些数据；你的系统备份（例如 iCloud 备份）可能仍保留较早的副本。游戏进度不会在设备之间同步。

English

Do Not Write Back does not send anything to us, or to anyone else. The app runs entirely offline and makes no network connections. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your phone

Everything the game remembers is stored on your device only:

  • the name you give the letters’ recipient
  • which letter you have reached, and the choices you made along the way
  • which reading you are on, and which endings you have unlocked
  • your language setting

The name is used only to address you within the story. It can be any name you like. None of this leaves your device, and we have no way to see it.

Removing the app removes this data; operating-system backups (such as iCloud Backup) may retain earlier copies. Progress does not sync between devices.

Purchases

The app is purchased through the App Store, and payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, your payment details, or your Apple Account.

Permissions

The app requests no system permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or notifications.

Support email

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use these only to reply to you.

Children

The app does not request personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date below.
